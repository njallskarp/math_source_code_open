# Prior-art positioning

## Primary mathematical context

1. Kotsireas and Winterhof introduced quaternary Legendre pairs, connected
   them to quaternary and binary Hadamard matrices, and developed compression
   and decompression constructions. Their paper supplies the base definition
   and normalization used here.

2. Jedwab and Pender gave the first general constructions at even lengths:
   length `(Q-1)/2` for prime powers `Q=1 mod 4`, and length `2p` for odd
   primes `p` when `2p-1` is a prime power. Neither construction covers
   length 42: `2*42+1=85` is not a prime power, and `42/2=21` is not prime.

3. Kotsireas, Koutschan, and Winterhof developed an even/odd separation of
   the search and cyclotomic-field PSD restrictions. Their 2024 preprint,
   published in 2025, identifies length 42 as the smallest unresolved even
   length after the then-known constructions.

4. Djokovic and Kotsireas provide the general periodic-complementary-
   sequence compression identity used as methodological background for the
   length-21 and length-7 reductions.

5. The 2025 compression paper by Kotsireas, Gómez, and Gómez-Pérez concerns
   binary Legendre pairs and fixed compressions. It is methodologically
   relevant but does not contain the present q=1/q=41 QLP-42 closure.

## Novelty claim made here

The defensible claim is narrow:

> Relative to the searched primary literature and committed Discovery Net
> graph through 2026-09-02, no prior source was found giving the exact
> exclusion of both extreme quarter-turn branches `q=1` and `q=41` in this
> canonical norm-32 QLP-42 shell.

This is apparent novelty, not proof of historical priority. The result does
not settle QLP-42, and it should not be described as a nonexistence theorem
for length 42.
