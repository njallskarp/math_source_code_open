# Three exact cyclic support families in the threshold-twelve closure addition

## Definition

For an edge ${a,b}$ of $K_{43}$ define its cyclic length by

$$
\ell(a,b)=\min(|a-b|,43-|a-b|)\in\{1,\ldots,21\}.
$$

Each persisted Cyclic(43) state is the set of edges toggled relative to the
fixed Exoo seed.  Its support signature is the multiset of cyclic lengths of
those toggled edges.  Rotation preserves this signature.

## Exact three-family classification

The complete 238-orbit threshold-twelve closure addition is the disjoint
union of exactly three support families:

$$
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r|r|r}
\text{non-length-one support}&|V|&\text{seeds}&\text{final shell}
&\text{components}&|E|&\text{cycle rank}\\\hline
\varnothing&190&183&7&56&166&32\\
\{5,16,16\}&38&38&0&3&58&23\\
\{17,17,21\}&10&8&2&2&8&0
\end{array}
$$

No other cyclic length occurs.  Equivalently, every one of the 238 states
uses only length-one toggles, except for exactly three non-length-one toggles
having one of the two rigid multisets above.

The exact total-support-size histograms are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varnothing &: 13^{28}14^{94}15^{58}16^9 17^1,\\
\{5,16,16\} &: 20^8 21^{18}22^{12},\\
\{17,17,21\} &: 16^4 17^6.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here, for example, $14^{94}$ means 94 states with 14 toggled edges.

## Component separation and shell placement

There are zero induced edges between distinct support families.  This also
follows structurally from the classification: a one-edge move changes the
support multiset in only one entry, while moving between any two displayed
non-length-one signatures requires changing at least three entries; cyclic
canonicalization cannot alter edge lengths.

The 38-state ${5,16,16}$ family consists of one 36-vertex component with 58
edges and cycle rank 23, together with two isolated vertices.  It contains no
final-shell state.

The ${17,17,21}$ family consists of two five-vertex trees.  Each tree has
four first-expansion seeds and one final-shell vertex.  These are precisely
the two final-shell vertices with addition degree two and three
objective-thirteen exits.

The remaining seven final-shell vertices lie in the length-one-only family,
where they join three seeds in a 10-vertex, 18-edge component of cycle rank
nine.  Each has addition degree four and eight objective-thirteen exits.

## Significance

The threshold-twelve addition is therefore confined to the union of three
very low-complexity support strata around the Exoo seed.  In particular, 190
of 238 orbits lie entirely in the 43-dimensional subcube generated by the
Hamiltonian cycle edges, while every other addition state differs from that
subcube in exactly three prescribed cyclic lengths.  This is a concrete
structural reduction for analyzing the local basin and designing targeted
higher-threshold searches.  It is not evidence that disconnected low-objective
components or the full objective-thirteen frontier obey the same restriction.

## Novelty and scope

The earlier boundary and closure contributions do not classify perturbation
supports by cyclic edge length.  This exact three-family partition and its
component separation are new refinements of the committed local closure
theorem.  They depend on the completeness of the 238-representative
certificate but use only transparent finite decoding and the independently
verified induced adjacency.

The result concerns the primary connected Cyclic(43) basin only.  It does not
exclude disconnected colorings with $M\leq12$, construct a
Ramsey(5,5,43) graph, determine $R(5,5)$, or improve its known bounds.
